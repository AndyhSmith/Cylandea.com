Balloon Bop

One balloon, one paddle, one tap. Keep it in the air as long as you can. It sounds simple, and for about ten seconds it is, then you catch a glimpse of the next balloon waiting to unlock. 15 balloons, each its own little world with its own physics, backdrop, and chiptune.

Download on theApp Store

Features

15 unlockable balloons

Each with its own gravity, sky, ground, and chiptune track, from the low-gravity moon balloon to the glitching scanline balloon.

10 achievements

Centurion, Marathon, Long Rally, Speedrun, Balloon Master and more to chase.

Game Center leaderboards

A board for every balloon, so you can see where your best run lands globally.

Play your way

Easy Mode adds side borders for a calmer run; Auto Aim or paddle-tilt aiming for full control.

Living worlds

Lava sparks, magnetic iron filings, rainy gliders, toxic bubbles, every balloon changes the scene.

No ads, no IAP

Pay once, play forever. Easy to learn, hard to put down.
