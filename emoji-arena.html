Emoji Arena

Three timeless strategy games in a single app, Tic-Tac-Toe, Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe, and Gomoku (Five in a Row), dressed up with emoji pieces, themed backgrounds, and an AI that adapts to how you play.

Download on theApp Store

Features

Three games in one

Classic Tic-Tac-Toe, the brain-bending Ultimate variant, and Gomoku, all in a single download.

Adaptive AI

Play a friend in two-player mode, or take on a single-player AI that ranges from Easy to Hard and adapts to your skill.

Emoji pieces

Swap the usual X's and O's for crowns, trophies, pandas and more, choose the icons you play with.

100+ backgrounds

Earn coins as you play and unlock over a hundred themed boards, from rainforests to retro diners.

Achievements & stats

Track your wins, streaks, and milestones across all three game modes.

Plays anywhere

Pass-and-play or solo, online or off. Quick to learn, satisfying to master.
