Taploom

Tap a tile. Watch the color ripple outward. Drain every tile on the board. Taploom is a calm, minimalist puzzle built around one simple idea, every tap sends a wave through the grid, and the art of solving each board in as few moves as you can.

Download on theApp Store

Features

55 hand-crafted levels

Eleven themed rows, each introducing a new tile that changes how the ripple flows.

A fresh daily puzzle

The same seed for every player worldwide, compare your solution against the world.

Endless mode

Seeded levels you can replay whenever you want one more.

Built-in level editor

Design, save and test your own puzzles with the full toolbox of tiles.

A growing toolbox

Amplifiers, mirrors, bombs, teleporters, prisms, locked tiles, infection tiles and more.

Three-star scoring

Clear the board, then chase the elegant minimum-move solution.

One-handed by design

Fully portrait, precise enough for a thumb on the bus, slow enough to think on the couch.

No ads, no tracking

No accounts, no in-app purchases, no internet required. Just the puzzle.

Also on Apple Watch

Taploom comes with a companion Apple Watch app, a focused, streamlined take on the puzzle built for quick taps on your wrist.

  • Endless puzzles: a steady stream of seeded boards to solve, one after another.
  • Pick your tiles: choose which special tiles can appear in your run.
  • Stars on the go: your watch runs track stars just like the phone.
  • Made for the wrist: crisp haptics on every tap and clear, no setup, just play.

The Watch app keeps things simple: the full 55-level campaign, daily puzzle, and level editor live on iPhone.
